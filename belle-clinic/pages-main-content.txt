# Acne Scar Treatments
![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/5HpvBL6E1BZ87nWbtMXvv9ZQ8o.jpg)
## Improve the Appearance of Acne Scars
We specialize in advanced acne scar treatments that aim to restore smoothness and clarity to your skin. Our personalized treatments address various types of scarring.
## Acne Scar Treatments
### BioRePeelCl3 Chemical Peel
The BioRePeelCl3 peel utilizes a potent blend of six acids—TCA, Lactobionic, Salicylic, Tartaric, Citric, and Aminobutyric acid (GABA)—along with amino acids and vitamins C and B2 to exfoliate the skin, stimulate collagen production, and promote cell turnover. This dual-phase treatment helps to diminish surface scars and reveal smoother, healthier skin beneath. **Our BiorepelCI3 starts at RM 350 per session for 6 sessions.**
### Fractional Erbium Glass Laser Treatment
The Fractional Erbium Glass Laser penetrates deep into the skin, creating controlled micro-injuries that trigger the body’s natural healing process. This stimulates collagen and elastin production, effectively reducing deeper acne scars, improving skin texture, and promoting a more even skin tone. **Our Fractional Erbium Glass Laser Treatment starts at RM 400 per session.**
### Gold Cell Skin Booster Injections
Our Gold Cell Skin Booster infuses the skin with nourishing ingredients, including hyaluronic acid and peptides, to deeply hydrate, plump, and rejuvenate the skin. This treatment further enhances the skin’s texture and tone. **The Golden Cell Skin Treatment starts at 1200 per session for 3 sessions.**
## Talk to Us to See If This Treatment Is Right for You
_Everyone we treat is unique._ Before starting any treatment, we require scheduling a consultation to determine if it is appropriate for you. Your health and safety are our top priorities. Our qualified professionals will assess your skin, needs, and medical history to ensure a treatment is suitable and to discuss any potential risks or benefits. Contact us today to book an appointment and learn more.
## Personalized Approach
Our specialists understand that everyone's skin is unique and provide treatments tailored to your skin's needs.
## Advanced Treatment Options
We offer a range of treatments, including laser therapy, microneedling, chemical peels, and dermal fillers, performed by our experienced team.

# Scars and Stretch Marks
![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/fgkkhUs7U5Nf61mY90fctBDh6Ms.jpg)
## Smooth and Restore Your Skin
Belle Clinic provides specialized treatments to minimize the appearance of scars and stretch marks. Whether dealing with acne scars, surgical scars, or stretch marks, our treatments promote skin regeneration and improve texture.
## Discover Scar and Stretch Mark Solutions
**Acne Scars**
Acne scars are permanent marks or indentations left on the skin after severe acne has healed. They can vary in appearance, including atrophic scars (depressed, such as ice-pick scars), hypertrophic scars (raised), or post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (dark spots). Treatments for acne scars focus on improving skin texture and color, often involving procedures like laser therapy, microneedling, or chemical peels.
**Surgical Scars**
Surgical scars are the result of incisions made during surgical procedures. These scars can vary in size, shape, and appearance, depending on the surgery and the body's healing process. While most surgical scars fade over time, some may remain prominent. Treatment options include laser therapy, topical treatments, and other procedures aimed at reducing the visibility of the scar and improving skin texture.
**Stretch Marks**
Stretch marks, also known as striae, are long, narrow streaks or lines that develop on the skin when it stretches or shrinks rapidly, such as during pregnancy, weight gain, or growth spurts. These marks are initially red, purple, or dark brown, and over time, they fade to a lighter, silvery color. Treatments to reduce the appearance of stretch marks include laser therapy, microneedling, and topical treatments designed to improve skin elasticity and tone.
**Keloid Scars**
Keloid scars are raised, thick, and often red or dark scars that extend beyond the original wound site. They occur due to an overproduction of collagen during the healing process and can continue to grow over time. Keloids are more common in individuals with darker skin tones and can be difficult to treat. Treatment options include steroid injections, laser therapy, and in some cases, surgical removal, though there is a risk of recurrence.
**Hypertrophic Scars**
Hypertrophic scars are raised scars that, unlike keloids, stay within the boundaries of the original wound. They result from an overproduction of collagen during the healing process but do not grow beyond the wound site. These scars may be red or pink and can become less noticeable over time. Treatments such as silicone sheets, steroid injections, and laser therapy can help flatten and reduce the redness of hypertrophic scars.

**Unwanted Facial Hair**
Unwanted facial hair refers to excessive or dark hair growth on areas of the face where it may be aesthetically undesirable, such as the upper lip, chin, or cheeks. This can be caused by hormonal imbalances, genetics, or certain medical conditions. Treatment options include laser hair removal, waxing, threading, or depilatory creams, which aim to reduce or eliminate hair growth for a smoother complexion.
**Unwanted Body Hair**
Unwanted body hair is the presence of excess hair on areas such as the arms, legs, back, chest, or bikini line, where it may be considered undesirable. Individuals may seek to remove or reduce this hair for aesthetic or comfort reasons. Common methods include laser hair removal, waxing, shaving, or the use of depilatory creams, with laser treatments offering longer-lasting results.
**Ingrown Hairs**
Ingrown hairs occur when hair curls back or grows sideways into the skin instead of rising up from the follicle. This can cause inflammation, pain, and sometimes small bumps or cysts, often in areas that are frequently shaved or waxed, such as the face, neck, underarms, or bikini line. Treatment focuses on preventing and alleviating ingrown hairs through proper hair removal techniques, exfoliation, and sometimes topical treatments to reduce inflammation and infection.
**Razor Bumps**
Razor bumps, also known as pseudofolliculitis barbae, are small, irritated bumps on the skin that develop after shaving, particularly in areas with coarse or curly hair. They are caused by hairs growing back into the skin after being cut too short, leading to inflammation, redness, and discomfort. Prevention and treatment strategies include using proper shaving techniques, soothing aftercare products, and in some cases, switching to alternative hair removal methods like laser therapy to reduce the occurrence of razor bumps.
